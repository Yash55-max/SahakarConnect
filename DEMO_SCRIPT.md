# SahakarConnect — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script
### SIH26089 (Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India)

Use this script during live judging or pitch presentations to demonstrate all core features and differentiators in **under 3 minutes**.

---

## ⏱️ Walkthrough Sequence

### Step 0: Open the Application (0:00 – 0:20)
1. Open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.
2. Note the **Top Demo Switcher Bar** that allows instant 1-click switching across all 4 stakeholder roles (**Consumer**, **Provider**, **Coop Admin**, **Regulator**).
3. Point out the live connection pulse indicating real-time Socket.io bi-directional sync.

---

### Step 1: The Consumer Experience & Transparent Booking (0:20 – 0:50)
1. Click **"1. Consumer (Rahul)"** in the top bar.
2. Browse the **Services Catalog**:
   - Filter by **Plumbing** or **Home Deep Cleaning**.
   - Show how each service card proudly displays **"PSCS Verified"** and the exact **Worker Take-Home Share** (e.g. ₹765 directly to Ramesh Yadav).
3. Click **"Book Service"** on *Complete Bathroom Leak & Pipe Fitting* (₹450):
   - Highlight the **Transparent Split Visualizer**:
     - 90% (₹405) to Worker
     - 8% (₹36) to Cooperative Health & Emergency Fund
     - 2% (₹9) to Platform Server/SMS
     - Compared with commercial aggregators taking 30% cuts!
   - Click **"Confirm & Book"**.

---

### Step 2: Real-Time Provider Inbox & Job Execution (0:50 – 1:30)
1. Click **"2. Provider (Ramesh)"** in the top bar.
2. Observe the **Real-Time Job Alert Toast** and the incoming job in the **Incoming Job Requests** section.
3. Show the **Duty Status Toggle**: Switch between *Available* and *Offline*.
4. Click **"Accept Job"**:
   - The job immediately moves to *Active & In-Progress*.
5. Click **"Start Job / On-Site"** followed by **"Mark Job Completed"**:
   - The deterministic payment service processes the simulated settlement.
   - The completed job moves into the **Completed Jobs & Transparent Ledger** table, instantly crediting the provider's earnings and welfare fund contribution.

---

### Step 3: The Cooperative Differentiator — Admin & Governance (1:30 – 2:20)
1. Click **"3. Coop Admin (Suresh)"** in the top bar.
2. Navigate the 4 tabs of the Cooperative Society Portal:
   - **Provider Verification Queue**: Review pending worker applications (e.g., Nitin Jadhav / Raju Naik) with mock Aadhaar KYC; click **"Approve & Verify Member"**.
   - **Transparent Split Ledger**: Show the macro visualizer and ledger table where every single rupee is mathematically accounted for.
   - **Welfare Fund & Aid**: Show the emergency reserves balance (₹1,45,200+) and transparent disbursement history (medical aid, tool modernization subsidies).
   - **Democratic Referendums (One-Worker-One-Vote)**: Show active polls on commission adjustments; click **"Create Member Referendum"** to demonstrate democratic member voting.

---

### Step 4: National Ministry Regulator Portal (2:20 – 2:50)
1. Click **"4. Regulator (Ministry)"** in the top bar.
2. Demonstrate the **National Registry & State Benchmarking Dashboard**:
   - Aggregate metrics: Total Registered Societies, Total Transaction Volume, National Welfare Reserves Pool, Verification Compliance Rate.
   - Comparative benchmark table across Delhi, Maharashtra (Pune), and Karnataka (Bengaluru).
   - Click **"Audit Drill-Down"** on *Delhi Shramik Sahakari Samiti* to view society disbursements and ledger compliance.

---

### Step 5: Wrap-up & Pitch Summary (2:50 – 3:00)
- *"SahakarConnect transforms the gig economy from extractive corporate middlemen into empowering, democratic worker cooperatives backed by the Ministry of Cooperation."*
