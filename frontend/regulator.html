Statutory Standards: Ministry of Cooperation Fair Gig Framework

SIH26089 Directive

1. Minimum 80% Net Payout

No intermediary or technology fee may deduct more than 20% total, of which at least 15% must remain within the primary cooperative society welfare corpus.

2. Mandatory Social Security

All registered PSCS societies must maintain continuous outpatient medical coverage and accident indemnity insurance for every active provider.

3. Cryptographic Governance Audit

All member votes, general body resolutions, and commission adjustments must be logged on the tamper-proof cooperative ledger with public verifiable receipts.
